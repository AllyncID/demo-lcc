# Hero background

Created with the built-in imagegen tool, using `public/assets/award.png` as a trophy/style reference.

Original: `public/assets/hero-background.png`.
Website asset: `public/assets/hero-background.webp` (1774 × 887, approximately 86 KB).

## Final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: responsive awards website hero background. Input image: reference for trophy identity, materials and black/gold lighting. Generate a new ultra-wide landscape composition, approximately 2:1 aspect ratio. Preserve the same elegant gold twisting aperture-shaped trophy with Sasak-inspired fine geometric engraving and round black marble plinth. Place the COMPLETE trophy on the RIGHT, centered at about 76% of image width, occupying about 78% of image height, with generous space above tip and below base. The left 55% must be extremely quiet dark charcoal negative space for website text. Seamless cinematic black and warm bronze-gold background, subtle amber glow behind trophy, soft very dark fabric across the bottom right fading toward the left. Full trophy and base sharply visible, photorealistic premium product photography. Outer edges fade into nearly uniform charcoal black #121211 with no visible frame, panel, sharp rectangular transition, or vignette cutoff. No green, olive or yellow-green cast. No text, typography, symbols, logos, people, watermark or UI. This is the image asset only, not a screenshot of a website.

## Responsive placement

The visual occupies the right column of the same 1280 px container used by the navigation and footer. Its image has a fixed 2:1 aspect ratio, scales with the visual height, and offsets the trophy to the column center. On narrow phones, the title/year occupy the left column, the trophy the right, and the description/actions span both columns. Text never becomes part of the raster asset. Edge masks blend the image into the existing black–gold gradient.
